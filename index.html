A few helpful answers

How do I play?

Tap a movable piece to select it. Tap the selected piece again, or use Turn, to rotate it clockwise. Match the openings to guide rain from the source to every seedling. Close the striped openings where rain would spill, then choose Make rain. Stone-marked pieces stay still. Undo and Reset garden are always available.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The puzzles and artwork are bundled with the app. Music and sounds are generated on the device. There are no downloads, accounts, advertisements, purchases, or online features in the game.

Why did my progress or sound settings reset?

The game intentionally keeps progress and settings only in memory for the current visit. Ending the app's process starts a fresh visit. Switching away may keep the visit alive, but the operating system can end a background process. Every garden is selectable without unlocking earlier ones.

How do I control music and sounds?

The first tap starts local audio. Music and Sounds have separate on/off controls. Check the device's volume and silent-mode settings if you cannot hear them. After switching back to the game, tap a control to resume audio. Sound is optional and is never the only way to understand a puzzle.

Does the game collect information?

The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no analytics, tracking, chat, or user accounts. Read the privacy policy for the separate handling of this website and optional public support messages.

For parents, guardians, and adult players

Need a hand?

Report a problem through the public GitHub support tracker. A GitHub account is needed to open an issue; it is not needed to play the game.

Issues are public. Include only the device model, iOS version, garden number, what happened, and what you expected. Do not post a child's name, age, location, contact information, identifying screenshots, passwords, or account tokens.
